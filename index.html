About the Author

Nitein K Shrivastav is a passionate professional, accomplished leader, and lifelong learner with decades of experience in manufacturing leadership and operational excellence. Throughout his career, he has mentored and inspired professionals by combining practical insights with grounded wisdom, empowering individuals and teams to achieve sustainable growth and excellence.

Driven by a strong belief that financial literacy is an essential life skill, he authored Educated Illiterates to simplify personal finance for everyday readers. Drawing from real-world experiences rather than complex theories, he shares practical financial lessons that help individuals make informed money decisions, develop healthy financial habits, and build a secure and prosperous future.

His mission is to inspire continuous learning, responsible financial decision-making, and purposeful leadership that creates lasting value in both personal and professional life.
