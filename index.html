Personal Financial Management Your Money, Your Future

Money is one of the most powerful forces in our lives. We work hard to earn it, yet many of us never really learn how to manage it. We are educated in schools and colleges, but when it comes to personal finance, many of us act like educated illiterates — we know how to spend, but not always how to save, protect, and grow our money. The chapters are simple, practical, and free from jargon. You won't find complicated formulas or heavy financial theories here. Instead, you'll discover stories, relatable examples, and easy-to-follow frameworks that can help you take charge of your money
